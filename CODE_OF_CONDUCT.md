# ScanBeforeEat Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Community Mission
ScanBeforeEat exists to empower people worldwide with transparent, evidence-grounded food ingredient intelligence. We cultivate an open, welcoming, and scientifically rigorous environment for developers, toxicologists, nutrition researchers, designers, and food science enthusiasts of all backgrounds.

---

## Expected Standards of Collaboration

We invite everyone participating in issues, pull requests, architectural reviews, or discussions to uphold these values:

- **Evidence-Based Technical Discourse**: Back food safety, additive assessments, and nutritional discussions with reputable, peer-reviewed public health bodies and published food regulations.
- **Empathetic & Courteous Communication**: Engage with respect and patience. Critique ideas and code implementations constructively, never attacking the person proposing them.
- **Inclusivity & Accessibility**: Foster an environment where individuals of every skill level, culture, background, and perspective can contribute meaningfully.
- **Transparency & Scientific Integrity**: Honestly disclose limitations in datasets, optical character recognition edge cases, or ingredient ambiguity. Avoid exaggerations or unfounded nutritional claims.

---

## Prohibited Behaviors

The following conduct violates our community guidelines and will not be tolerated in any forum associated with this project:

- Harassing, derogatory, discriminatory, or exclusionary comments or behavior.
- Personal slights, trolling, insults, or unconstructive antagonization.
- Deliberately publishing misleading health advice, fabricated toxicity claims, or unverified scare tactics.
- Sharing private personal data, contact information, or sensitive credentials without unambiguous prior consent.
- Unsolicited commercial advertising or off-topic spam.

---

## Community Moderation & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for stewarding a safe and honest collaborative atmosphere. When violations arise, maintainers will review the incident objectively and take proportional steps, which may include clarifying guidelines, removing problematic comments, requesting revisions, or restricting repository access.
