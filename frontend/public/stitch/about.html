Why We Engineered a Public Food Defense System

ScanBeforeEat began when a team of bioengineers and distributed systems architects observed a systemic flaw in modern nutrition apps. Modern grocery aisles are saturated with ambiguous pseudoscientific buzzwords: "All Natural," "Clean Label," and "Plant-Derived."

Behind these soothing claims often lie industrially altered emulsifiers, hidden hyper-concentrated sodium compounds, and neuro-active flavor enhancers linked to metabolic dysfunction.

Worse yet, investigation revealed that numerous legacy nutrition databases and consumer scanning utilities receive covert sponsorships, licensing subsidies, or advisory board funding from consumer packaged goods conglomerates.

We resolved to rebuild the stack entirely: zero brand sponsorship, strict algorithmic independence, and continuous toxicological indexing mapped directly to gold-standard academic and clinical literature.

Algorithmic Transparency: How We Score

Our open calculation model penalizes industrial degradation without subjective bias. Every deduction is traced directly to biological and metabolic impact thresholds.

Initial Baseline

Standard Base Score: 100 / 100

Unprocessed, singular whole foods in their natural matrix start with full structural integrity.
