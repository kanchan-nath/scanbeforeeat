Your dietary preferences, scanned barcodes, and nutritional telemetry are fundamentally personal. Here is our exact data handling blueprint.

01 Information We Collect expand_more

Barcodes scanned (EAN/UPC) and packaging ingredient list OCR text submitted directly via camera. We perform zero background location tracking, zero contacts syncing, and zero invasive device fingerprinting.

02 How We Use Data expand_more

Exclusively to run deterministic toxicological scoring algorithms, map items against European additive E-number databases, calculate ultra-processed food indices (NOVA classification), and cache verified ingredient profiles.

03 Third-Party Services expand_more

Open Food Facts API: Open-source public food telemetry catalog for barcode matching.

NVIDIA AI Inference Cluster: Ephemeral server-side OCR parsing of complex multilingual packaging.

Razorpay / Stripe: PCI-DSS certified payment processors for scanning tier passes.

04 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliant) expand_more

Under GDPR Articles 15-22 and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to a full export in standardized JSON/CSV format, immediate data portability, instant deletion, and the unconditional right to object to automated classification.

05 Cookies & Local Storage expand_more

Zero third-party advertising cookies or canvas beacons. We employ strictly functional browser IndexedDB storage and local tokens to allow rapid offline grocery store barcode caching and preserve dietary restriction filters.
