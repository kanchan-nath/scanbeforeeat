Our Privacy Commitment

We never sell your scan history or dietary data to third parties.

Information We Collect

When you use ScanBeforeEat, we collect information to provide accurate food ingredient analysis and improve your overall experience. This includes account details such as your email address and profile preferences when you register.

Additionally, we log scan history, custom dietary restrictions, allergen alerts you configure, and basic technical diagnostics to ensure application stability and performance.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized to process ingredient barcodes and text labels against your personal dietary profiles, deliver real-time allergen warnings, and maintain your personal scanning ledger.

We may also analyze anonymized and aggregated scanning trends to improve our global food database accuracy and enhance core application features.

Data Storage & Security

We implement enterprise-grade encryption standards both in transit and at rest. All user credentials and sensitive preference parameters are securely hashed and stored within isolated cloud infrastructure.

Our security protocols undergo routine evaluations to protect against unauthorized access, data alteration, disclosure, or accidental destruction.

Third-Party Services

ScanBeforeEat integrates with trusted infrastructure partners for cloud hosting, authentication, and error reporting. These entities are contractually bound to maintain strict confidentiality and are prohibited from utilizing your personal data for independent marketing.

Cookies

We utilize essential cookies and similar local storage mechanisms to maintain session continuity, remember your interface settings, and analyze anonymous traffic patterns to ensure dependable app operation.

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Depending on your geographic jurisdiction, you hold specific rights regarding your personal information. These include the right to access, correct, export, or restrict the processing of your personal data at any time.
