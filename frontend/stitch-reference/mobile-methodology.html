Algorithmic Architecture

4 Pillars
01

Structural / NOVA

Quantifies degree of industrial food processing based on the internationally recognized NOVA classification framework.

02

Chemical & Regulatory

Analyzes synthetic additives, pesticides, and contaminants against EFSA, FDA, and independent toxicological limits.

03

Nutrient Density

Measures ratio of essential micronutrients, vitamins, and fiber relative to total caloric load.

04

Clinical & Epidemiological

Incorporates longitudinal health outcome studies regarding long-term metabolic and inflammatory markers.

calculate

Scoring Formula & Weight Calculation

Final Safety Score (S) is calculated using a weighted baseline minus penalty deductions for harmful additives and processing extremes:

S = max(15, min(95, round(70 + B_whole - Deductions)))additive × Severity) - (ΔNOVA × 15)

Additive Risk Classification

5 Tiers
Tier 1 // Safe Status: Approved

Benchmark: Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C)

Biological concern: Negligible. Acts as a potent antioxidant with established safety profiles.

Tier 2 // Low Risk Status: Regulated

Benchmark: Citric Acid

Biological concern: Generally safe, minor dental enamel erosion risk at high concentrations.

Tier 3 // Moderate Status: Restricted ADI

Benchmark: Sodium Benzoate

Biological concern: Potential mild inflammatory triggers in sensitive subsets when combined with ascorbic acid.

Tier 4 // High Risk Status: Under Review

Benchmark: Artificial Dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5)

Biological concern: Associated with behavioral hyperactivity in pediatric studies and mild gut barrier disruption.

Tier 5 // Severe Hazard Status: Banned / Phased Out

Benchmark: Partially Hydrogenated Oils (Trans Fats)

Biological concern: Direct correlation with cardiovascular disease, systemic inflammation, and LDL cholesterol spikes.
