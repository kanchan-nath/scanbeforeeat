The Foundation

Four-Pillar Algorithmic Architecture

Every packaged item scanned by ScanBeforeEat is dissected across four discrete clinical dimensions before arriving at a composite safety score.

science Pillar 01 // Structural

NOVA Food Processing Classification

University of São Paulo 4-Tier Standard

We determine whether a product retains its cellular matrix or consists of fractionated extracts reconstituted with cosmetic agents. Analyzes extreme mechanical extrusion, hydrolysates, invert sugars, and fractionated lipids.

Primary Focus: Matrix integrity Max Penalty: -35 pts
warning Pillar 02 // Chemical

Toxicological & Regulatory Profiling

EFSA, WHO/JECFA, ANSES, US FDA GRAS Cross-Reference

Cross-checks every E-number and chemical pseudonym against global regulatory advisories. Evaluates chronic bioaccumulation risk, genotoxicity markers, banned statuses in European jurisdictions, and acceptable daily intake (ADI) limits.

Primary Focus: Synthetic Additives Max Penalty: -40 pts
pie_chart Pillar 03 // Metabolic

Nutrient Density & Macro Balance

Enhanced French Nutri-Score Derivation

Evaluates caloric concentration relative to bioactive nutrients. Penalizes free fructose, trans-fat isomers, and hidden sodium load while rewarding essential dietary fiber, intact plant proteins, and intrinsic polyphenol concentration.

Primary Focus: Caloric Quality Max Penalty: -25 pts
neurology Pillar 04 // Clinical

Clinical & Epidemiological Evidence

PubMed, Cochrane & Gut Microbiome Cohort Synthesis

Continuous natural language ingestion of high-impact medical journals. Flags emerging evidence regarding gut barrier disruption (zonulin increase), non-caloric artificial sweetener impacts on glucose tolerance, and endocrine perturbation.

Primary Focus: Emerging Health Trials Bonus/Penalty: +/-10 pts
Algorithmic Breakdown

Scoring Formula & Weight Allocation

Toxicological Framework

5-Tier Additive Risk Classification Matrix

Every authorized and non-authorized food additive is categorized into five transparent threat levels determined by consensus in peer-reviewed biochemical literature.

Tier Level Risk Badge Regulatory Status Benchmark Examples Biological Mechanism of Concern
Tier 1 Safe EFSA Authorized / FDA GRAS Ascorbic acid (E300), Citric acid (E330), Rosemary extract Naturally metabolized; essential organic nutrients or benign endogenous cellular compounds.
Tier 2 Low Risk Authorized with high ADI threshold Guar gum (E412), Apple pectin (E440), Steviol glycosides Mild gastrointestinal fermentation in high amounts; inert polysaccharide chains.
Tier 3 Moderate Subject to regulatory re-evaluation Sodium benzoate (E211), Xanthan gum, Maltodextrin Potential in vivo benzene formation with ascorbic acid; spikes blood glucose indices.
Tier 4 Precaution Restricted in EU; Warning labels mandatory Carrageenan (E407), Polysorbate 80 (E433), BHA / BHT Disruption of protective intestinal mucus layer, bacterial translocation, and endocrine disruption.
Tier 5 Severe Hazard Banned in EU / ANSES Monograph Caution Titanium dioxide (E171), Potassium bromate, Azodicarbonamide Genotoxicity markers, nanoparticle accumulation in organs, potential carcinogen classifications.
