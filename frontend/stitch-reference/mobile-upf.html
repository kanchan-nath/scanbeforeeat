The 4 NOVA Groups

Global benchmark developed by University of São Paulo

info
NOVA 1

Unprocessed Foods

check_circle Baseline

Fresh fruits, vegetables, raw nuts, whole grains, eggs, and raw meats without additives.

STATUS: DAILY ESSENTIAL
NOVA 2

Culinary Ingredients

balance Moderate

Oils, butter, lard, raw cane sugar, maple syrup, and sea salt extracted directly from nature.

STATUS: USE TO SEASON WHOLE FOODS
NOVA 3

Processed Foods

check Acceptable

Canned beans with salt, artisanal cheeses, freshly baked simple sourdough bread, cured fish.

STATUS: COMBINE WITH GROUP 1
NOVA 4

Ultra-Processed (UPF)

warning Restrict

Industrial extrusion, synthetic azo dyes, emulsifiers, cosmetic flavors, hyper-palatable formulations.

STATUS: CRITICAL RISK FACTOR

5 Rules to Spot UPF

Scan ingredient statements with these criteria

1

Industrial Pantry Disconnect

Look for 5+ ingredients you wouldn't find in a regular home kitchen cupboard.

2

Synthetic Simple Carbohydrates

High fructose corn syrup, dextrose, invert sugar, maltodextrin, and hydrogenated starches.

3

Cosmetic Azo Food Colorants

Yellow 5 (Tartrazine), Red 40, Sunset Yellow, and Titanium Dioxide added solely for visual appeal.

4

Industrial Emulsifiers & Thickeners

Polysorbate 80, Carboxymethylcellulose (CMC), mono- and diglycerides, and modified starches.

5

Abnormal Shelf Life Resilience

Baked or prepared foods engineered not to spoil or decompose for 12 to 24+ months.
